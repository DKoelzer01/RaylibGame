# Ore vein families (#1467, the underground plan #1465): a deposit is one family, a main ore and its byproducts (real-ish
# geology, GregTech style). PlanetField picks a family per deposit cell among those whose hosts and depth fit, by rarity x
# biome x region, then a member per point by weight (orefamilies.h). Format agreed with Lighting (2026-11-22).
# All numbers GUESS until the playtests.
#   members: ores.txt ids with weights (floats), the main ore first
#   hosts:   rock strata it sits in (rock, granite, basalt, sandstone, limestone, marble), or any. Hard: a deposit whose
#            centre is in another rock skips the family; a cell with no family that fits has no deposit
#   depth:   min max m under the original surface ("-": no limit)
#   biomes:  rarity multipliers by surface biome (unlisted x1), or any
#   regions: rarity multipliers by underground region (#1547: none, karst, volcanic, frozen, crystal, flooded, fungal)
#   rarity:  its weight among the families that fit a cell
#   shape:   blob (today's deposits), sheet (a dike or vein plane), pipe (vertical), seam (a flat bed), stockwork (a mesh of
#            thin veins); extents (sheet span, pipe height, seam length) are fixed per shape in code, inside the 96 m cell
#   size:    min max m, rolled per deposit: blob or pipe radius, sheet or seam thickness, stockwork vein spacing
# id               | members                              | hosts                    | depth  | biomes                          | regions                 | rarity | shape     | size
