# Controls
WASD walk, Shift run, Space jump. In the air, Space and Ctrl fire the jetpack up and down (it refills on the ground, which costs suit power).
LMB mines, digs and chops; hold LMB on a built part to break it. RMB places the loose ground (dirt, sand, gravel) in your hand.
1-0 pick hotbar slots: an empty slot is bare hands. Hover an item in the inventory and press a number to put it on that key.
Tab opens the inventory (crafting, quests, the quest tree, the suit, this manual). G toggles build mode. T looks through the telescope. L switches the headlamp. F5 saves.
Looking at a machine: RMB opens it, I puts matching items in, O takes the outputs. On farmland: I plants seeds, O harvests.
With a flight pack fitted, H toggles hover.

# Survival
Four bars: oxygen, suit power, health and food. Terra's air is breathable; under water, in caves without air and in space your oxygen drains (a rebreather slows it under water).
Suit power drains slowly, faster with the headlamp on, and pays for refilling the jetpack. Oxygen canisters and power cells on the hotbar top the bars up.
Food: press its hotbar key once to hold it, again to eat it. Cold weather makes you hungrier (insulated suits help); heat hurts without a thermal regulator.
Falls faster than about 6 m/s hurt (servo legs raise that). Dying sends you back to your spawn.

# Tools and wear
Your first tools are stone: pick up loose stones with LMB (1-2 stone each) and craft a stone pickaxe, axe or hoe in the Crafting tab.
Tools wear with use and show a warning when nearly worn out; repair them in the inventory before they break. A tool that breaks leaves your hotbar.
Better tools (bronze, iron, steel...) dig harder rock and work faster.

# Building
G enters build mode. The mouse wheel picks the shape, R and V rotate it, RMB places, holding LMB removes a part.
Parts are built from the material in your hand (stone, planks, concrete...); machines are placed from the hotbar like blocks.
Parts snap to a 0.5 m grid on a build plot, which starts where you place the first part. B saves the plot you look at as a blueprint; N cycles your blueprints.

# Machines and power
Early machines burn fuel (a campfire, a kiln, the coke oven). The steam boiler needs fuel and water: fill it with buckets, a hand pump placed against it, or a water pump's pipes.
Generators make power for electric machines through cables. Power comes in tiers (LV, MV, HV...): a machine needs a supply of its own tier.
Ore processing (macerator, ore washer, centrifuge) gets more metal out of each ore. The quest tree shows what each tier unlocks.

# Water
The sea, rivers and large lakes never run dry. Ponds are finite: dig a channel from one and it drains.
Water flows into holes and channels you dig next to it. Buckets scoop and pour 1 cubic metre.
Pumps lift water from under them into pipes; outlets pour it out and boilers fill from them. A hand pump (no power) pumps while you hold RMB on it.

# Farming
Till soil with a hoe to make farmland. Plant seeds on it (I) and harvest ripe crops (O). Fields near water grow better; buckets and outlets can water them.

# Animals
Animals live in herds. Hold a food an animal eats and it follows you; lead it into a pen of fences and gates (you can walk through a gate, animals can't).
Some animals are dangerous, mostly at night. Keep your distance or build walls.

# The map and the telescope
T looks through the telescope: zoom in to see far terrain, the moon and the ring. The map item shows the land around you and what you have found (not on Hard).

# Flight pack
An MV suit module (crafted from an MV hull, good circuits and a thruster pack). Fit it in the Suit tab, then H toggles hover: you hold your height with no drift, WASD moves (Shift is faster), Space and Ctrl climb and descend.
Hovering drains suit power, faster when boosting. A charged flight pack catches you if you fall too fast.

# Rockets and space
Rockets are built from parts: engines, fuel tanks, a cockpit, a guidance computer and landing legs. The moon and the asteroid ring hold materials Terra doesn't; the quest tree shows the way there.
