# Tiers (quest book): tier | name | gate item (making it opens the next tier) | what to do
0 | Primitive        | fired_crucible   | Chop trees for wood, harvest bushes for fibre, dig dirt and stone. Make clay from dirt, mould a crucible (Tab) and fire it in a campfire (G to place, I to feed it wood).
1 | Stone age        | bronze_ingot     | Build a kiln. Dig deep for copper and tin ore (veins below 8 m), smelt them in the kiln (fuel: wood, charcoal, coal) and alloy 3 copper + 1 tin into bronze.
2 | Bronze and steam | steel_ingot      | A boiler and copper cables power steam machines (8 V). Hammer plates, crush ore, bake coal into coke in a coke oven, and burn coke in a blast furnace to turn iron into steel.
3 | Steel            | vacuum_tube      | Glass (sand in a kiln), copper wire and a steel plate make a vacuum tube: the first circuit.
4 | LV electric      | electric_furnace | Basic circuits on a steel hull make your first LV machine. Power it from an LV generator (32 V; steam machines refuse it).
5 | MV electric      | aluminium_ingot  | Process ore for more: a macerator crushes it (x2), an ore washer and a water pump purify it, a centrifuge splits off byproducts (iron: nickel, copper: gold). MV (128 V): an MV generator powers an electrolyzer (water: hydrogen and oxygen) and a chemical reactor (polyethylene for good circuits). Electrolyse bauxite dust into aluminium. A miner works the deposit under it.
6 | HV electric      | titanium_alloy_ingot | HV (512 V): an HV generator, an arc furnace (ilmenite and coke into titanium; titanium and aluminium into titanium alloy), a refinery (rocket fuel) and a precision assembler (alloy plates, advanced circuits).
7 | Rocketry         | cockpit          | Build a rocket on a plot: rocket engines under fuel tanks, a guidance computer and a cockpit on top (landing legs soften touchdowns). Load rocket fuel (I), board (E), throttle up (Shift) and fly to orbit: 35 km up and fast sideways.
8 | Space            | -                | The ring 50 km up (the huge rocks goliath, bastion and pebble), then the moon.
